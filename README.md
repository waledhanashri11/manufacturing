# S&S Corporation - Official Enterprise Web Platform

Official website for **S&S Corporation**, a multi-disciplinary industrial enterprise uniting heavy manufacturing infrastructure, piping fabrication, and process automation with specialty water treatment chemistry under one unified roof.

## Operating Businesses

1. **S&S Engineering Solutions**
   - Industrial Process Piping (SS304, SS316, Carbon Steel, MS)
   - ASME Process Vessels, Storage & Mixing Tanks
   - Modular Dosing & Pumping Skids
   - Plant Automation, PLC & SCADA Systems
   - AODD, Metering & Progressive Cavity Pumps & Valves
   - Turnkey Plant Erection & Commissioning

2. **AquaCorp Chemicals**
   - Boiler Water Treatment Regimens (Oxygen Scavengers, Scale & Corrosion Inhibitors)
   - Cooling Tower Chemistry (Biocides, Biodispersants, Anti-Scalants)
   - Reverse Osmosis (RO) Membrane Antiscalants & Cleaning Chemicals
   - Effluent & Wastewater Treatment (ETP/STP Coagulants, Flocculants, De-coloring Agents)
   - Integrated Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) Water Scaling Calculator
